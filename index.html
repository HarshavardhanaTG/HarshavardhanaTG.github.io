Harsha Gowda

Engineering systems at the intersection of human sensing and machine intelligence.

Research Publications CV

I engineer technologies that augment and restore human abilities, guided by a deep understanding of human behavior. My current work centers on peripheral neuromotor interfaces, with applications ranging from restoring communication for individuals with severe speech disorders to building intuitive neuromotor interfaces for virtual environments. My expertise spans neuroengineering and machine learning, with a focus on understanding and creating embodied intelligence.

I am currently a graduate student in the Computer Engineering Department at the University of California, Davis. Before this, I built satellites at the Indian Space Research Organisation, and I completed my undergraduate studies at the Indian Institute of Space Science and Technology, specializing in satellite communication and navigation systems.
