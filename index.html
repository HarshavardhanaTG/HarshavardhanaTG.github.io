Harsha Gowda

Engineering systems at the intersection of HUMAN SENSING and MACHINE INTELLIGENCE.

I'm a graduate student in the Electrical and Computer Engineering department at the University of California, Davis. I engineer end-to-end systems at the intersection of human sensing and machine intelligence, inferring the neuromotor, visual, and kinematic intent behind human actions to build systems that let people interact more fluidly with physical and virtual environments.

Before UC Davis, I earned a Bachelor's degree in Avionics from the Indian Institute of Space Science and Technology and worked at the Indian Space Research Organization, where I helped build and launch satellites.
