Harsha Gowda

Engineering systems at the intersection of human sensing and machine intelligence.

Research Publications CV

I engineer technologies that augment and restore human abilities, guided by an understanding of human behavior that shapes seamless human-computer interaction and human-inspired embodied systems. My current work centers on peripheral neuromotor coordination, with applications ranging from restoring communication for individuals with severe communication disorders to building intuitive neuromotor interfaces for virtual environments. More broadly, my research sits at the intersection of neuroengineering, machine learning, and human behavior.
